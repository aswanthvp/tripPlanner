      Key 2027 dates

      Oktoberfest, Munich: 18 Sep – 3 Oct 2027. Amsterdam Dance Event: 20–24 Oct 2027. Swiss high passes (Grimsel, Furka) usually close for winter between late Oct and Nov, so check before you go.

      Driving

      Carry your Indian licence + International Driving Permit. Dropping a rental car in a different country usually costs hundreds of euros, so loop back or split into one car per country. Switzerland needs the CHF 40 motorway vignette (rentals there include it).

      About the numbers

      Distances are straight-line × 1.25 for roads; times are estimates (drive ≈ 80 km/h incl. breaks, train ≈ 110 km/h, flights include airport time).
